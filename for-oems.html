Security and your data.

Your OEM data stays yours. Straight answers on the questions procurement always asks.

Tenant isolation. Every OEM knowledge base is scoped by tenant ID. Your service manuals and brochures never mix with another builder's corpus, and cross-tenant queries are impossible by design (RLS + scoped API keys). If you end the engagement, we return your data and delete our copy within 30 days.

Training on your data. We do not train foundation models on your content. Your corpus powers retrieval only. If we ever change that, it requires a signed addendum from you first.

Infrastructure. Production-hardened API. Rate limiting per-IP and per-session, timing-safe admin auth, CORS locked in production, signed webhooks. Data hosted in US-East by default, EU (Frankfurt) available on request for GDPR-scoped builders.

Compliance today. DPA available on request. Standard MSA with confidentiality + IP assignment clauses. SOC 2 Type I in progress (target Q2). If your procurement needs SOC 2 Type II before signing, we can discuss timing.

Content rights. Every document in your knowledge base is either OEM-approved, publicly licensed, or fair-use with a cited source. No copyrighted manuals scraped from forums.

Liability & answer accuracy. Every answer carries a source citation your owners can click. The MSA includes standard hold-harmless language for owner interpretation of advisory answers. E&O coverage details in the contract.

Questions procurement always asks.

Who owns the model and the knowledge base?

You own your content and the derived knowledge base. We own the platform code. If you leave, you get an export of your corpus and any structured intervals + failure patterns we derived from it. The AI runtime itself stays with us.

Can we self-host later if we want to?

Yes, under a separate license tier. Self-hosted deployments run on your infra with our runtime image, and you continue to get monthly knowledge updates. Most builders start hosted with us for the first 12 months to move fast, then decide.

What data do we need to hand over?

Service manuals per model line, sales brochures, service bulletins if you have them, standard interval schedules, and a technical contact who can answer factory questions during ingest. That's the minimum. Fault-code decoders, warranty databases, and dealer network directories improve depth but aren't required to launch.

What happens when the engineer gets an answer wrong?

Every reply carries a source citation and an inline "not helpful" signal owners can tap. Flagged threads land in a weekly review queue that we triage together with your team. Systematic misses feed back into the knowledge base within the next monthly update cycle. Safety-critical topics get a lower confidence threshold and route straight to your dealer network.

How is this different from putting ChatGPT on our site?

Generic LLMs guess with confidence. This engineer is scoped to your corpus, refuses to guess outside it, cites its sources, and knows your model line down to serial ranges and part numbers. It also handles the marine-specific state machine (vessel identification, fault-code decoding, escalation to dealer) that a generic chat wrapper doesn't.
