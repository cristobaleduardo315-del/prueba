Is there a free tier?

Yes. Ask the engineer without an account, or sign up free with your email + boat info to save your thread and use the app across days. Paid plans unlock unlimited chat, the full dashboard, and the signed inspection log.

Do I need a credit card to try it?

No. Try the engineer as a guest with no signup. Free membership only asks for email + your boat's make, model, and year so answers get more specific. Card is only required when you're ready to upgrade.

Is there a free trial?

Fleet Management includes a 14-day trial with every paid feature: unlimited chat, the full dashboard, saved inspection log with PDF export, and multi-vessel views. Cancel any time in your account and you're not charged. Yacht Master starts immediately with the same cancel-anytime policy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your account in one click. Your inspection log and vessel profile stay accessible; you just drop back to the free tier. No support call, no retention offer wall.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your vessel profile, inspection log, and chat threads stay on your account and remain exportable. If you delete your account, everything is removed within 30 days per our privacy policy.

Do you offer a Fleet discount?

Fleet Management covers up to 5 vessels at $299/mo. Larger fleets (10+) get a custom quote. Reach us at hello@yachtmastershub.com.
